framecode example

Exact timestamps for 29.97 fps video.

This page renders ten seconds of animation at 30000/1001 fps and encodes it in your browser with WebCodecs. Each frame’s timestamp and duration come from FrameClock.

Fixed durations drift.

At 30000/1001 fps a frame lasts 33,366.67 µs. Rounding that once to 33,367 µs and adding it for every frame makes each frame a third of a microsecond too long.
